Hosting Side Projects on a Phone
Why I run several bots and services out of Termux on an old Samsung S10, and what that setup actually looks like.

Why a phone
A spare Android phone is a computer that's already paid for, already has a battery backup, and is already online. For a handful of lightweight Node.js and Python bots, that's enough.

The setup
Termux gives a real Linux userland on Android. Combined with a process manager and a cron-based watchdog, it's stable enough to run several bots continuously without a dedicated server.

Trade-offs
This isn't a setup for anything that needs guaranteed uptime or heavy compute. It's a setup for validating ideas cheaply before deciding whether something deserves real infrastructure.

What moves to the cloud
Once a project has real users or needs to scale past what a phone can handle, it moves to a proper host. The phone is a proving ground, not a permanent home.
